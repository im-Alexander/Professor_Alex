What is the Advanced Format?

In short the Advanced Format is just the current pokémon trading card game standard format with a banlist.
The goal of the Advanced Format is to reign in the power level of the top decks, and limit the number of degenerate cards so that some more fun and interesting decks can shine!
The Banlist will be updated on a frequent basis (Usually after a new set is released or after a major tournament) so that the format stays fresh.
